Sun

The Sun is a massive ball of hot plasma, containing 99.86% of the solar system's mass. It's about 4.6 billion years old and converts 600 million tons of hydrogen into helium every second through nuclear fusion.

Fun fact from Glorp

The Sun is so big that about 1.3 million Earths could fit inside it! And its light takes 8 minutes and 20 seconds to reach Earth. That means when you look at the Sun (don't actually look directly at it!), you're seeing it as it was 8 minutes ago!

About SolarScope

SolarScope is an interactive educational journey through our solar system. Explore planets, stars, moons, and more with your cosmic guide, Glorp the alien cat!

Our mission is to make learning about space fun, engaging, and accessible for curious minds of all ages. From the blazing Sun to the icy outer planets, discover the wonders of our cosmic neighborhood.
